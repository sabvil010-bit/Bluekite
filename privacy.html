The small print

Privacy policy

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Here is exactly what that means.

The short version

Bluekite is built to be safe for students. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking cookies. You can use every part of the site without telling us anything about yourself.

What we don't collect

  • We don't ask you to sign up or log in.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or social media trackers.
  • We don't set cookies.
  • Your quiz answers are checked inside your own browser and are never sent to us or saved.

If you contact us

If you email hello@bluekite.space, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply, and we delete messages once the conversation is finished, or after 12 months at the latest. We never add you to a mailing list, and we never sell or share your details.

Students under 13 should ask a parent, carer or teacher before contacting us. You never need to tell us your full name, age or school.

Technical information

Like any website, our hosting provider may briefly keep basic technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the site secure and working. We don't use these logs to identify or follow anyone.

Fonts

Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google's servers. No cookies are set by this.

Your choices

You can ask us to see or delete any message you have sent us by emailing hello@bluekite.space. A parent, carer or teacher can also ask on a student's behalf.

Changes

If we ever change how Bluekite handles information, we'll update this page and the date below. Last updated: January 2026.
